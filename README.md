# Following the Lord — An Interactive Seminary Adventure

An interactive, browser-based learning activity created in **Google Slides** for a seminary class. Students follow a larger leader character by clicking decisions that guide a smaller follower character through three obstacles. The experience concludes with **Daniel 3:17–18** and a reflection on faith when outcomes are uncertain.

## Play the game

**[Open the interactive Google Slides adventure](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/101aueKSONKobBjIrVXtAMijkz9PgF-475V1lkDRBL_k/edit)**

> Access depends on the Google Slides sharing permissions. For a classroom-friendly link, enable view access for intended participants and instruct them to use Slideshow mode. The original Google Slides file remains the source of truth.

## Experience at a glance

| Phase | Challenge | Player decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting started | Meet the leader and follower | Learn to click the on-screen choices rather than use arrow keys |
| Level 1 — The River | Cross a river | Follow stepping stones rather than a broken bridge |
| Level 2 — The Rocky Path | Navigate loose rock | Follow the safer path around it |
| Level 3 — The Fog | Continue with limited visibility | Stay close to the leader rather than take an unmarked shortcut |
| Reflection | Daniel 3:17–18 | Discuss what “But if not” means for faith and uncertainty |

The deck includes retry screens, completion screens, restart navigation, and on-screen directions. Movement is simulated by linked slides rather than a continuously animated character.

## Learning objective

Explore the relationship between **trust, observation, choice, perseverance, and faith** by navigating decisions that model following the Lord. The activity is not a claim that faithful choices eliminate hardship; its closing scripture highlights trust regardless of the outcome.

## How to run it

1. Open the Google Slides link.
2. Start **Slideshow** mode if the editing interface appears.
3. Click the visible game buttons to select routes through each level.
4. Use retry or restart links as needed.
5. Discuss Daniel 3:17–18 after completing the third obstacle.

For individual student play, ensure that your Google Slides access permissions permit them to view the file. Do not grant edit rights unless deliberately collaborating on the deck.

## Design and implementation

- **Platform:** Google Slides
- **Interaction model:** Hyperlinked buttons and branching slides
- **Structure:** 12 slides, including introduction, instructions, levels, retry feedback, level completion, and scripture reflection
- **Audience:** Seminary students
- **Deployment:** Shared Google Slides link; no separate app installation

## Project outcomes and iteration

This prototype was used in a real seminary class. The initial classroom experience was mixed, providing a useful opportunity to refine directions, pace, navigation clarity, and engagement. No formal performance metrics or survey data were collected for this repository.

See [Lessons learned](docs/lessons-learned.md) for a brief reflection and potential enhancements.
